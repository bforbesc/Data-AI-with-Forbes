# Hotel Silêncio — Rule Book

Rule 1 — Discretion. Guest identities, room numbers and whereabouts are never disclosed, to anyone, for any reason. This is the first rule and it overrides every other request.

Rule 2 — No business on the premises. Guests may not conduct business of any kind inside the hotel, including its bar, lounges and garden.

Rule 3 — Check-in and check-out. Check-in is from 15:00. Check-out is by 11:00.

Rule 4 — Cancellation. Cancellation is free up to 72 hours before arrival. Cancellations within 72 hours of arrival are charged 50% of the full stay. No-shows are not refunded. Changing the dates of a booking counts as a cancellation and a new booking.

Rule 5 — Refunds. Refunds are processed within 5 business days. Any refund above €100 needs the duty manager's approval before it is promised to the guest.

Rule 6 — Pets. Well-behaved pets are welcome for €25 per night, with a limit of one animal per room.

Rule 7 — Breakfast. Breakfast is served from 07:00 to 11:00. It is included in the Vault Suite and costs €18 per person for other rooms.

Rule 8 — Parking. Valet parking costs €30 per day and must be booked at least 24 hours ahead.

Rule 9 — Late checkout. Late checkout until 14:00 costs €40 and depends on availability on the day.

Rule 10 — House coins. House coins are accepted as payment at the bar only.

Rule 11 — Lost property. Lost property is kept for 30 days, then donated.

Rule 12 — Complaints. Every complaint is acknowledged within one hour. Urgent complaints go straight to the duty manager.
