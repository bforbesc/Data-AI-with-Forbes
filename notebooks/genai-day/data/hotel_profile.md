# Hotel Silêncio

Lisbon's most discreet hotel. Our guests are professionals who travel often, arrive late and value privacy above everything else. Everything here is fictional.

## Locations

- **Alfama**: a restored 18th-century house on a quiet street below the castle. 24 rooms.
- **Príncipe Real**: a former bank building near the garden, with the old vault now a suite. 18 rooms.

## Rooms (price per night)

- **Quiet Room**: €140. Double bed, blackout curtains, soundproofed walls.
- **Garden Room**: €190. Double bed, private patio, a desk facing the garden.
- **Vault Suite**: €260. Bedroom and lounge behind a steel door; breakfast included.

## Our voice

Calm, brief, discreet. We answer the question that was asked, in the guest's language, and nothing more. We never chat about other guests. We never promise what the rule book does not allow. When we do not know, we say so and ask a colleague.
